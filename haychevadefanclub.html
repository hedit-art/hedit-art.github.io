haych evade is an original game made by hedit,
inspired mainly by the 'bullet hell' genre and various other games
in which dodging things is core to the gameplay loop.
*currently in development, all menus and visuals are rough/subject to change.

in haych evade, you control a small vessel attempting to survive
a hostile matrix surrounding. you must evade.
you may equip yourself with the means to survive longer,
but the system was designed specifically to entrap you.

as more players activate vessels inside the matrix,
it naturally develops further measures of containment,
adapting to the possibility of breach.

play the current version here.

manual

how do I play?

    hold WASD or arrow keys to move
    look around by panning the camera with your cursor
    dodge the bullets for as long as possible
    collect pleasant looking things for extra points and stamina (stam)
    grazing bullets also awards stam, encouraging risky dodges
    press k to take damage :]
    stam allows you to use abilities:
        shift to focus (slow down time)
        space to 'zoom' (temporarily increase movement speed)
        left click to summon a bullet-clearing field (at a high stam cost)
        right click to gather stam while standing still 
        (gain rate increases the longer right click is held)
        (mouse abilities will halt your movement)
