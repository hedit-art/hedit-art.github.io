haych evade (original game)

  • in development, all menus and visuals are rough/subject to change
  • try the OPEN PLAYTEST VERSION here
  • how to play:
    hold WASD or arrow keys to move
    look around by panning the camera with your cursor
    dodge the bullets for as long as possible
    collect pleasant looking things for extra points and stamina (stam)
    grazing bullets also awards stam, encouraging risky dodges
    stam allows you to use abilities:
        shift to focus (slow down time)
        space to 'zoom' (temporarily increase movement speed)
        left click to summon a bullet-clearing field (at a high stam cost)
        right click to gather stam while standing still 
        (gain rate increases the longer right click is held)
        (mouse abilities will halt your movement)
